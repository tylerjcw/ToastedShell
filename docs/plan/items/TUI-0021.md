## Problem

There is no padding, no margin and no alignment on any widget. A caller who wants a
dialog's text inset by one column adds spaces to the string; one who wants a title
centred computes the offset and pads both sides. `TuiBorder` insets by exactly one cell
because that is what a border is, and nothing else insets at all.

Lipgloss — the styling half of Bubbletea — treats padding, margin, alignment and border as
properties of a style that any renderable carries, and it is the reason Bubbletea layouts
read as short as they do. Rich has `Padding` and `Align` as wrappers.

## Acceptance

- [ ] `Padding` on `TuiWidget`, in the CSS order, affecting measure and arrange
- [ ] `HorizontalAlignment` and `VerticalAlignment` for a widget smaller than its slot
- [ ] `TuiBorder`'s inset becomes padding rather than a special case in its draw
- [ ] The markup spellings follow: `Padding = 1`, `Padding = "1 2"`, `Align = "center"`
- [ ] `TuiOverlay` centres its modal through alignment rather than its own arithmetic

## Notes

Measure has to account for it or a padded widget is clipped rather than inset, which is
the bug this always ships with the first time.
